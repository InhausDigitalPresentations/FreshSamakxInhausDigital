The shift

Treat it as a launch, not a refresh.

The brand has been here for years, but the city is meeting it for the first time. So we introduce it properly.

Goal 01

Own “fresh” in Abu Dhabi

Become the authority people quote when they talk about fresh fish: where it comes from, when it's best, how to tell.

Goal 02

Make the offering obvious

Fresh fish to your door, and fish cooked by us. Clear, easy and one tap away, ordered direct, not through an app.

The idea

Know who caught it.

Every brand says fresh. Only one can tell you who caught your fish, on which boat, this morning.

Freshness becomes provenance. Tradition becomes proof. And the people of the sea, the most authentic asset Abu Dhabi has, become the face of the brand.
